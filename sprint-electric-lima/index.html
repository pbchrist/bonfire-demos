A Lima company for more than 35 years

Todd Spicer founded Sprint Electric in 1989 with Phillip Brooks, starting out in a leased building. The company has since grown into its own 12,600 square foot main office and warehouse on East Hanthorn Road in Lima.

Today Sprint Electric is owned by Bryan Brooks and Andrew Burbacher, and serves commercial and industrial customers in the Lima and Columbus markets.
