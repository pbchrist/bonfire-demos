A union mechanical contractor

Gartman Mechanical Services is a mechanical contractor for process manufacturing, HVAC, refrigeration and plumbing. Our service department maintains and repairs high pressure boiler systems, chillers, make up air systems and much more.

Gartman is a union shop that requires a five year apprenticeship, plus 2,000 hours of day school, 700 hours of night school and 10,000 hours of work. Our residential division grew from customers who wanted that same crew on their home systems.

  • Licensed professional engineer on staff
  • Sheet metal & pipefitting journeymen
  • State certified plumbers
  • Authorized Trane dealer
